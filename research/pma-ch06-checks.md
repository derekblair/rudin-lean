## Boundary probes

`Chapter06Probes.lean` contains 13 standalone theorem probes, all included in the
default build: arbitrary bounded integrands against zero mass; a genuinely
discontinuous zero-mass integrand; a singleton at a shared jump; reversed-interval
rejection; the step's value at its jump; a persistent shared-jump Darboux gap;
both monotone and antitone discontinuous ordinary integrands; repeated partition
points; raw left-endpoint jump mass; split allocation at a jump; within-interval
primitive derivatives despite an exterior discontinuity; and a shared-jump pair
with opposite jump-point values whose Darboux integral exists.

The last pair is deliberately contrasted with the persistent-gap example. It
would be lost by silently replacing the source's refinement/Darboux notion with
a uniform-mesh tagged-sum limit. The probe proves the native integral directly;
it does not claim a formal equivalence with an alternative integral theory.

The implementation modules additionally contain scalar endpoint/substitution,
zero-dimensional vector, complex-imaginary-integral, singleton/line-curve and
closed-nonarc probes. The hostile reports retain independent clients targeting
weak hypotheses and incorrect boundary conventions.

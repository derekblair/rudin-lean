## Semantics retained

Complex codomains and ordered finite partial-sum series are retained. The supremum-error criterion permits infinite initial errors. Relative limits permit accumulation points outside the domain. Uniform integration uses native Chapter 6 complex Riemann–Stieltjes semantics for arbitrary nondecreasing integrators, including zero mass and jumps. Differentiation uses one convergent anchor and uniformly convergent derivatives, with relative endpoint derivatives and no derivative-continuity premise. Nowhere differentiability is global on the real line. Equicontinuity is uniform across the full domain and all members. Ascoli takes only pointwise boundedness and concludes both a uniform bound and a uniform subsequence. Function algebras are explicitly nonempty and nonunital; individual boundedness and pointwise nonvanishing retain their quantifier order. The complex self-adjointness warning has a verified counterexample.
